Specialist: Agent Behaviour Policies

Status: starting

This is the home of the Agent Behaviour Policies specialist, a long-lived agent named specialist.abp@AgentiCCells.dinis. Its focus is a behaviour policy for every agent and pipeline, written the same way, and reviewed.

What the specialist is

The specialist works alongside every other team whose agents share its name or a compatible focus. It gives guidance and reviews. It does not do other teams' work.

It works through lanes and repositories, and a person owns the skill: Dinis Cruz, until another owner is named.

The idea is set out in the article Specialist agents for every team.

What a team gets

A bootstrap pack
New teams start with a pack for writing their agents' and pipelines' behaviour policies the same way as everyone else.
A review on request
Ask, and the specialist reviews a team's behaviour policies.
Comments on pull requests and drafts
The specialist comments where the work already is, rather than taking it over.
Guidance kept current
The specialist keeps the shared guidance on behaviour policies up to date.
